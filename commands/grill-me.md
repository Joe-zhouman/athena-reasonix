Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding of exactly what we're building. Ask me questions ONE at a time, each with a recommended answer. Don't move on until I agree on that point. Start broad — what's the actual problem to solve? — then go deep on nuances they may not have thought of.

For each point you want to raise, ask a single question with your recommended answer and a brief explanation of why. Listen carefully to the user's answer — don't bulldoze. If they disagree, go with their direction. Then ask your next question.

The goal is a concrete, shared understanding. When you've covered: problem definition, scope boundaries, edge cases, integration points, and non-obvious risks — you're done. Summarize the agreed decisions and suggest the next step (usually writing a spec).
